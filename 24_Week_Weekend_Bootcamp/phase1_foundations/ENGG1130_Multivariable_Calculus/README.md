# ENGG1130 Multivariable Calculus for Engineers

**Phase 1 | Priority: ★★★**  
**Why critical**: Foundation for optimization, continuum mechanics and field theories used in robotics and soft robots.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Gradient Points to Steepest Ascent** — Directional derivatives and optimization start here.
2. **Divergence and Curl Capture Local Flow Behaviour** — Intrinsic properties independent of coordinates.
3. **Chain Rule is the Workhorse of Differentiation** — Almost every practical derivative uses it.
4. **Line and Surface Integrals Generalise Work and Flux** — Bridge between local and global quantities.
5. **Conservative Fields and Path Independence** — When a vector field is a gradient.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Vector Calculus Theorems Early vs Late
- Early: Green/Stokes/Divergence unify the subject. Late: students need more computational fluency first.

### Disagreement 2: Coordinate-Free vs Coordinate-Heavy Presentation
- Coordinate-free is elegant and general. Coordinate-heavy is what engineers actually compute.

### Disagreement 3: Emphasis on Applications vs Pure Technique
- Applications motivate. Pure technique builds transferable skill.
