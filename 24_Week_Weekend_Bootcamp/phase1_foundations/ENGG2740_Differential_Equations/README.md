# ENGG2740 Differential Equations for Engineers

**Phase 1 | Priority: ★★★**  
**Why critical**: Language of dynamics, control, heat transfer, vibrations and continuum models.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Order and Linearity Determine Solution Structure** — Superposition holds only for linear equations.
2. **Homogeneous + Particular Solution** — General solution of linear non-homogeneous equations.
3. **Characteristic Equation and Eigenvalues** — Link between ODEs and linear algebra.
4. **Phase Plane Reveals Qualitative Behaviour** — Especially valuable for nonlinear systems.
5. **Boundary-Value vs Initial-Value Problems** — Different existence and uniqueness character.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Analytic Solutions vs Qualitative / Numerical Emphasis
- Analytic builds exact understanding. Qualitative and numerical are what engineers use for real systems.

### Disagreement 2: How Early to Introduce Nonlinear Systems
- Early: most real systems are nonlinear. Later: linear theory must be solid first.

### Disagreement 3: Laplace Transform as Primary vs Secondary Tool
- Primary: solves many engineering problems efficiently. Secondary: can hide underlying dynamics.
