---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Energy is Conserved (1st Law)** — But not all energy is equally useful.
2. **Entropy Never Decreases in an Isolated System (2nd Law)** — Gives directionality to processes.
3. **State Functions vs Path Functions** — Internal energy depends only on state; heat and work do not.
4. **Availability / Exergy** — The useful portion of energy relative to the environment.
5. **Idealizations Bound Real Performance** — Ideal gas, reversible processes, etc., give upper limits.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Classical Macroscopic vs Statistical Microscopic First
- Classical is sufficient for most engineering. Statistical explains the “why” of entropy.

### Disagreement 2: Emphasis on Cycles vs Pure Properties
- Cycles are the application. Properties are the foundation.

### Disagreement 3: SI vs Other Unit Systems in Teaching
- SI is coherent and modern. Other systems still appear in some industries.
