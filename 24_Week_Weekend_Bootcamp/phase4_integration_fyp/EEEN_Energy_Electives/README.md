## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Energy is Conserved; Exergy is Not** — Quality of energy matters as much as quantity.
2. **Power Density and Energy Density Trade Off** — Batteries, capacitors and fuels serve different roles.
3. **Intermittency Requires Storage or Flexibility** — Generation and load must be balanced in time.
4. **System Efficiency is Multiplicative** — Losses at each conversion stage compound.
5. **Lifecycle and Resource Constraints Bound Sustainability Claims** — Cradle-to-grave analysis matters.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Centralised vs Distributed Energy Systems
- Centralised: economies of scale. Distributed: resilience and local optimisation.

### Disagreement 2: Which Storage Technology Wins for Mobile Robots
- Batteries dominate today. Fuel cells, hybrids and future chemistries compete for niches.

### Disagreement 3: How Much Energy Content Belongs in a Robotics Curriculum
- Essential for mobile and field robots. Can be left to specialists for fixed industrial arms.
