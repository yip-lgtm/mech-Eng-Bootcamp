## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **A Good Problem is Half the Solution** — Clear scope, measurable success criteria and realistic constraints prevent wasted effort.
2. **Literature Review is Active, Not Passive** — You read to find gaps, methods, baselines and pitfalls, not just to summarize.
3. **Risk-Driven Planning** — Identify the hardest technical uncertainties early and attack them first.
4. **Documentation is Part of the Work** — Future you (and examiners) need a clear record of decisions, data and rationale.
5. **Iteration is Expected** — The proposal will evolve; build in review points rather than pretending the plan is final.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Ambitious Novel Research vs Solid Engineering Demonstration
- **Research**: higher risk, higher potential contribution. **Demonstration**: higher chance of a complete, polished system.

### Disagreement 2: How Much Simulation vs Hardware in the First Semester
- **Simulation-heavy**: reduces early hardware cost and risk. **Hardware-early**: discovers integration issues sooner.

### Disagreement 3: Individual Depth vs Breadth of System Integration
- **Depth**: strong technical contribution in one area. **Breadth**: a complete working system that impresses holistically.
