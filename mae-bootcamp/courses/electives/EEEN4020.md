**CUHK MAE | Other Elective | 3 units | Stream: Energy**

## Official Description

This course covers the scientific and engineering fundamentals of solar energy and photovoltaic technology. Topics include solar radiation resource, solar geometry, solar thermal collectors, photovoltaic cell physics, semiconductor fundamentals, p-n junction, solar cell performance parameters (efficiency, fill factor, open-circuit voltage), module and array design, power electronics for PV systems, grid-connected and off-grid PV systems, solar desalination, and economic analysis of solar energy systems.

# 問題 1：5 個核心心智模型 (5 Mental Models)

## Model 1: 太陽能幾何學與輻照資源 (Solar Geometry & Irradiance Resource)

**Core equations**:

$$\mathrm{AM} = \frac{1}{\cos\theta_z}, \quad G_{\mathrm{sc}} = 1367 \,\mathrm{W/m^2} \;(\mathrm{AM0}), \quad G_{\mathrm{STC}} = 1000\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\;(\mathrm{AM1.5})$$

$$\sin\alpha_s = \sin\varphi\,\sin\delta + \cos\varphi\,\cos\delta\,\cos\omega$$

$$\cos\omega_s = -\tan\varphi\,\tan\delta \;\Rightarrow\; H = \frac{2}{15}\cos^{-1}(-\tan\varphi\,\tan\delta)$$

$$I_T = I_b R_b + I_d R_d + I_g R_r$$
(beam, diffuse, ground-reflected components on tilted surface; Liu & Jordan 1963)

The tilt factor for beam on a south-facing surface:
$$R_b = \frac{\cos(\varphi-\beta)\cos\delta\cos\omega_s' + \sin(\varphi-\beta)\sin\delta}{\cos\varphi\cos\delta\cos\omega_s + \sin\varphi\sin\delta}$$

and the Hay-Davies diffuse transposition (anisotropic sky):
$$I_{dT} = I_d \cdot \left[\frac{G_{\mathrm{b}}}{G_0} R_b + \left(1-\frac{G_{\mathrm{b}}}{G_0}\right)\frac{1+\cos\beta}{2}\right]$$

**Constants and reference numbers**:
- Solar constant $G_{\mathrm{sc}} = 1367 \,\mathrm{W/m^2}$ (ASTM G173; Kopp & Lean 2011 measured 1360.8 ± 0.5 W/m² during solar minimum)
- Extraterrestrial spectrum AM0: 1366.1 W/m² integrated (ASTM E490)
- Hong Kong GHI annual: ≈ 1400–1600 kWh/m²/yr (HKO 2020)
- Peak Sun Hours (PSH): ≈ 3.5–4.5 h/day in Pearl River Delta
- Extraterrestrial declination amplitude: ±23.45°; equation of time amplitude ±16 min (Spencer 1971 Fourier series)

**Key scholars & dates**:
- Liu, B.Y.H. & Jordan, R.C. (1963). "The long-term average performance of flat-plate solar-energy collectors." *Solar Energy*, 7(3), 106–114. — *Decomposition of total irradiance into beam/diffuse; this is the foundation of tilted-surface transposition*.
- Duffie, J.A. & Beckman, W.A. (2013). *Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes*, 4th ed., Wiley. — *The standard textbook for the equations above*.
- Iqbal, M. (1983). *An Introduction to Solar Radiation*, Academic Press. — *Detailed treatment of anisotropy models*.
- Spencer, J.W. (1971). "Fourier series representation of the position of the sun." *Search*, 2(5), 172. — *Closed-form solar declination*.
- Hay, J.E. & Davies, J.A. (1980). "Calculation of the solar radiation incident on an inclined surface." *Proc. First Canadian Solar Radiation Data Workshop*, 59–72. — *Anisotropic diffuse model*.
- Kopp, G. & Lean, J.L. (2011). "A new, lower value of total solar irradiance: Evidence and climate significance." *Geophys. Res. Lett.*, 38, L01706.
- ASTM G173-03(2020). "Standard Tables for Reference Solar Spectral Irradiances."

**中英對照 (Bilingual)**: 太陽能工程嘅第一步永遠係 determine **solar geometry** — 即係太陽相對於地面觀測者嘅位置。Declination angle $\delta$ 由赤道平面同地球-太陽連線之間嘅�角定義，喺 ±23.45° 範圍內按年週期變化（夏至 +23.45°, 冬至 −23.45°）。Hour angle $\omega$ 將時間映射至天空，每 15° = 1 小時。香港位於 $\varphi = 22.3°$ N, 所以夏天正午 solar altitude 達 85°+, 最佳 tilt = $\varphi - 15° \approx 22°$（Duffie 嘅 "latitude minus 15°" rule for high-summer load profile）。呢個雙重時空幾何學埋咗喺兩個 fundamental equations 裏面。

## Model 2: 光伏電池物理與 Shockley-Queisser 極限 (Photovoltaic Cell Physics & S-Q Limit)

**Core equations**:

$$\eta_{\mathrm{SQ}}^{\max} \approx 33.7\%, \qquad E_g^{\mathrm{opt}} \approx 1.34\,\mathrm{eV}\;(\mathrm{GaAs})$$

$$I = I_L - I_0\!\left[\exp\!\left(\frac{qV}{nkT}\right) - 1\right] - \frac{V}{R_{\mathrm{sh}}}$$

$$V_{\mathrm{oc}} = \frac{nkT}{q}\ln\!\left(\frac{I_L}{I_0} + 1\right)$$

$$\mathrm{FF}_0 \approx \frac{v_{\mathrm{oc}} - \ln(v_{\mathrm{oc}} + 0.72)}{v_{\mathrm{oc}} + 1}, \qquad v_{\mathrm{oc}} = \frac{qV_{\mathrm{oc}}}{nkT}$$

The detailed-balance current density from reciprocity:
$$J = q\int_{E_g}^{\infty}\frac{2\pi E^2}{h^3c^2}\left[\frac{1}{e^{E/kT_\mathrm{sun}}-1} - \frac{\Omega}{e^{E/kT_\mathrm{cell}}-1}\right]\mathrm{d}E$$

where $\Omega = e^{qV/kT_\mathrm{cell}}$ is the luminescence escape factor under bias.

**Loss-budget for silicon** (Green 2012 loss diagram; updated by Richter 2013):

| 損耗機制 Loss mechanism | Δη (% of incident) |
|---|---|
| Below-bandgap (E<Eg) transmission | ~19% |
| Above-bandgap thermalization | ~33% |
| Carnot (T_cell vs T_sun) | ~1% |
| Boltzmann (V_oc vs E_g/q) | ~5% |
| Radiative recombination | ~1% |
| Non-radiative (Auger) | ~3% |
| Voltage factor / FF / Rs | ~9% |
| **Remaining η** | **~29.4%** |

**Key scholars & dates**:
- Shockley, W. & Queisser, H.J. (1961). "Detailed balance limit of efficiency of p-n junction solar cells." *J. Appl. Phys.*, 32(3), 510–519. — *The foundational paper; established the 33.7% single-junction limit under 1-sun AM1.5*. This was the **1687**-equivalent moment for photovoltaics: Newton's *Principia* set mechanics; Shockley-Queisser set the thermodynamic ceiling.
- Shockley, W. (1949). "The theory of p-n junctions in semiconductors and p-n junction transistors." *Bell Syst. Tech. J.*, 28, 435–489. — *pn-junction theory*.
- Green, M.A. (1982, 3rd ed. 2014). *Solar Cells: Operating Principles, Technology and System Applications*, UNSW. — *Loss-decomposition framework*.
- Tiedje, T., Yablonovitch, E., Cody, G.D. & Brooks, B.G. (1984). "Limiting efficiency of silicon solar cells." *IEEE Trans. Electron Devices*, ED-31(5), 711–716. — *Auger-limited Si efficiency ~29%*.
- Richter, A., Hermle, M. & Glunz, S.W. (2013). "Reassessment of the limiting efficiency for crystalline silicon solar cells." *IEEE J. Photovoltaics*, 3(4), 1184–1191. — *Revised Si Auger limit ~29.4%*.
- Yoshikawa, K. et al. (2017). "Silicon heterojunction solar cell with interdigitated back contacts for a photoconversion efficiency over 26%." *Nature Energy*, 2, 17032. — *World record Si cell 26.7%*.
- Roos, A. & Boreland, M. (2018). "The grand challenges of photovoltaic technology." *IEEE J. Photovoltaics*, 8(2), 363–372.

**Bilingual**: Shockley-Queisser 嘅 detailed balance 假設每個高於帶隙嘅光子只產生**一個**電子-空穴對，剩餘能量 ($\Delta E = h\nu - E_g$) 通過 phonon emission 變成熱能；低於帶隙嘅光子則穿透太陽電池。呢兩個 loss channel 已經限制咗 single-junction 嘅 theoretical maximum ~33%。Si 嘅 $E_g$ = 1.12 eV 比 optimal 1.34 eV 低，所以 Si cell 永遠 "pay a tax" of ~2-3 absolute % efficiency; 呢個係 GaAs (1.42 eV, 更接近 optimum) 一直保持 single-junction record (33.9% lab) 嘅根本原因。

## Model 3: 溫度、串聯電阻與填充因子 (Temperature, Series Resistance & Fill Factor)

**Core equations**:

$$\mathrm{FF}_0 \approx \frac{v_{\mathrm{oc}} - \ln(v_{\mathrm{oc}}+0.72)}{v_{\mathrm{oc}}+1}, \quad v_{\mathrm{oc}} \equiv \frac{qV_{\mathrm{oc}}}{nkT}$$

$$\mathrm{FF} \approx \mathrm{FF}_0 \left(1 - r_s\right), \quad r_s \equiv \frac{R_s}{V_{\mathrm{oc}}/I_{\mathrm{sc}}}$$

$$\frac{1}{\eta}\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial T}\bigg|_{\mathrm{Si}} \approx -0.0040 \,\mathrm{K^{-1}}, \quad \frac{\partial V_{\mathrm{oc}}}{\partial T} \approx -2 \,\mathrm{mV/K}$$

$$R_{\mathrm{s,total}} = R_{\mathrm{contact}} + R_{\mathrm{bulk}} + R_{\mathrm{grid}} + R_{\mathrm{emitter}}$$

$$\eta_{\max} = \frac{\mathrm{FF} \cdot V_{\mathrm{oc}} \cdot I_{\mathrm{sc}}}{P_{\mathrm{in}}} = \frac{\mathrm{FF} \cdot V_{\mathrm{oc}} \cdot J_{\mathrm{sc}}}{G_{\mathrm{STC}}}$$

Five-parameter single-diode model (more accurate than two-diode):
$$I = I_L - I_0\left[\exp\!\left(\frac{q(V+IR_s)}{nkT}\right) - 1\right] - \frac{V+IR_s}{R_{\mathrm{sh}}}$$

Lumped-parameter thermal model for cell temperature:
$$T_{\mathrm{cell}} = T_{\mathrm{amb}} + \frac{\mathrm{NOCT}-20}{0.8} \cdot G_{\mathrm{STC,frac}}$$

where NOCT (Nominal Operating Cell Temperature) ≈ 45±2°C for standard modules at $G = 800\,\mathrm{W/m^2}$, $T_{\mathrm{amb}} = 20°C$, wind 1 m/s.

**Practical numbers**:
- Premium Si cell FF = 0.80–0.85; degradation above R_s > 0.5 Ω·cm²
- Mono-Si Pmax temperature coefficient: −0.35 to −0.45 %/K
- PERT/PERC/TOPCon typical V_oc: 680–740 mV
- HJT world record: V_oc ≈ 750 mV, FF ≈ 0.847 (Yoshikawa 2017)
- T = 60°C operation in Hong Kong summer: ≈ 25–35% relative efficiency penalty (using η·(1 − 0.004·35) = 86% of STC)

**Key scholars & dates**:
- Green, M.A. (1982). *Solar Cells*, UNSW Press.
- Swanson, R.M. (1986). "Point-contact solar cells: Modeling and experiment." *Silicon Processing for Photovoltaics*, North-Holland. — *Detailed FF analysis*.
- Wolf, M., Noel, G.T. & Stirn, R.J. (1977). "Investigation of the double exponential in the current–voltage characteristics of silicon solar cells." *IEEE Trans. Electron Devices*, ED-24(4), 419–428. — *Two-diode model*.
- McIntosh, K.R. (2001). "Lumps, humps and bumps: Three detrimental effects in the current–voltage curve of silicon solar cells." *PhD Thesis, UNSW*. — *R_s, R_sh, and breakdown effects*.
- Skoplaki, E. & Palyvos, J.A. (2009). "On the temperature dependence of photovoltaic module electrical performance." *Solar Energy*, 83(5), 614–624. — *Comprehensive temperature-coefficient review*.
- Chan, D.S.H. & Phang, J.C.H. (1987). "Analytical methods for the extraction of solar-cell single- and double-diode model parameters from I–V characteristics." *IEEE Trans. Electron Devices*, ED-34(2), 286–293. — *Standard parameter-extraction algorithms*.

**中英對照**: 太陽電池嘅 **fill factor (FF)** 係 maximum power 同 $I_{sc} V_{oc}$ 嘅比值，本質係衡量 I-V 曲線「矩形」程度嘅指標。practical FF = 70–85%，單純因為 recombination losses 同 series resistance $R_s$ 嘅 power dissipation $\Delta P_{R_s} = I^2 R_s$ 而降低。喺 Hong Kong 夏天，光伏組件 cell temperature 可達 60–70°C，每升高 10°C，效率約下降 4%。NOCT = 45°C 係 module datasheet 嘅 standard reference, real-world temperature derating 必須透過 local climate inputs (Tamb_avg, G_max, wind speed) 計算 $T_{\mathrm{cell}}$ 嘅 hourly distribution 然後 weighted-average 個 efficiency。呢個係**thermal derating** 嘅 main factor，同時解釋咗為何香港比內蒙古沙漠 ground-mount PV 嘅 capacity factor 低約 15%。

## Model 4: 最大功率點追蹤與電力電子 (MPPT & Power Electronics for PV)

**Core equations**:

MPPT condition:
$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial V} = 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \frac{\partial I}{\partial V} = -\frac{I}{V}$$

P&O update rule:
$$V_{\mathrm{ref}}^{(k+1)} = V_{\mathrm{ref}}^{(k)} + \Delta V \cdot \mathrm{sign}\!\left(\frac{\Delta P}{\Delta V}\right)$$

IncCond exact condition (no oscillation at steady state):
$$\frac{\Delta I}{\Delta V} = -\frac{I}{V} \quad (\mathrm{at\ MPP})$$

Boost converter CCM:
$$\frac{V_{\mathrm{out}}}{V_{\mathrm{in}}} = \frac{1}{1 - D}, \quad D \equiv \mathrm{duty\ cycle}$$

SEPIC (buck-boost):
$$\frac{V_{\mathrm{out}}}{V_{\mathrm{in}}} = \frac{D}{1 - D}$$

Switching losses:
$$P_{\mathrm{sw}} \approx \frac{1}{2} f_{\mathrm{sw}} V_{\mathrm{sw}} I_{\mathrm{sw}} (t_{\mathrm{on}} + t_{\mathrm{off}})$$

Inverter efficiency model:
$$\eta_{\mathrm{inv}} = \frac{P_{\mathrm{out}}}{P_{\mathrm{out}} + P_{\mathrm{loss}}^{\mathrm{cond}} + P_{\mathrm{loss}}^{\mathrm{sw}} + P_{\mathrm{loss}}^{\mathrm{aux}}}$$

Conduction loss for a MOSFET:
$$P_{\mathrm{cond}} = I_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 R_{\mathrm{DS,on}}$$

**Transformer-less H5 inverter topology** (Patrao et al. 2011; SMA Sunny Boy):
- Adds 5th MOSFET in DC link to decouple AC side from PV array during zero-crossing
- Eliminates reactive-current circulation → efficiency 97.5–98.0% (vs H-bridge with grid-frequency transformer: 94–95%)
- Common-mode leakage current $I_{\mathrm{cm}}$ suppressed to < 30 mA (DIN VDE 0126-1-1)

**HERIC topology** (Highly Efficient and Reliable Inverter Concept; Phoenix Solar/Schmidhuber-era patent):
- AC-side bidirectional switch (two IGBTs anti-series) creates zero-voltage "freewheel" during zero-crossing
- Decouples PV from grid → same leakage suppression as H5
- Efficiency: 97.0–98.0% EU weighted

**Key scholars & dates**:
- Esram, T. & Chapman, P.L. (2007). "Comparison of photovoltaic array maximum power point tracking techniques." *IEEE Trans. Energy Convers.*, 22(2), 439–449. — *Canonical MPPT review*.
- Kjaer, S.B., Pedersen, J.K. & Blaabjerg, F. (2005). "A review of single-phase grid-connected inverters for photovoltaic modules." *IEEE Trans. Power Electron.*, 20(5), 1292–1306.
- Hua, C. & Shen, C. (1998). "Study of maximum power tracking techniques and control of DC/DC converters for photovoltaic power system." *IEEE PESC*.
- Liu, F. et al. (2008). "A variable step size INC MPPT method." *IEEE Trans. Power Electron.*, 23(3), 1163–1170. — *Improved dynamic response*.
- Hussein, K.H. et al. (1995). "Maximum photovoltaic power tracking: an algorithm for rapidly changing atmospheric conditions." *IEE Proc.-Gener. Transm. Distrib.*, 142(1), 59–64. — *P&O foundational paper*.
- Schmidt, H., Burger, B. & Schmid, H. (2003). "Wechselrichter mit hohem Wirkungsgrad und Verfahren zur Verbesserung des Wirkungsgrades." Patent DE102004037343 (HERIC). — *HERIC topology invention*.
- Patrao, J.C.G. et al. (2011). "Transformerless topologies for grid-connected single-phase photovoltaic inverters." *Renew. Sustain. Energy Rev.*, 15(7), 3423–3444.

**State machine of MPPT**:

| Region | $\partial P/\partial V$ | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Left of MPP | $> 0$ | Step V up |
| Right of MPP | $< 0$ | Step V down |
| At MPP | $\approx 0$ | Hold (oscillates for P&O; clean hold for IncCond) |

**Bilingual**: 最大功率點追蹤 (MPPT) 解決咗光伏嘅 "**non-linear current source**" 嘅實際問題：因為 panel 嘅 I-V curve 隨 irradiance 同 temperature 變動，operating point 必須實時調整以追逐 MPP。現代 three-level inverters (例如 Huawei SUN2000) 採用 **Perturb-and-Observe + adaptive step-size**，可達 99% peak EU efficiency。Transformerless topology (H5, HERIC) 取代傳統 low-frequency transformer (效率損失 2–4%), 但代價係 common-mode leakage current 必須受控 (VDE 0126-1-1 限值 30 mA)。 現代 string-inverter 結合 MPPT + grid filter + anti-islanding, EU weighted efficiency 可達 98.5%。

## Model 5: 聚光光伏、多接面電池與超越 S-Q 極限 (CPV, Multi-junction & Beyond-SQ)

**Core equations**:

Concentration ratio: $C \equiv G_{\mathrm{concentrated}}/G_{\mathrm{direct}}$

Detailed-balance multi-junction limit (Araujo 1989; Fraas 2015):
$$\eta_{\mathrm{n-junc}}^{\max} \to \infty\text{-junction limit} \approx 86.8\% \text{ under full concentration}$$

For $n$ junctions with bandgaps $E_{g,i}$, current-matched:
$$\eta_n = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n q V_{\mathrm{mpp},i} J_{L,i}}{\int_{0}^{\infty} E\,\phi_{\mathrm{sun}}(E)\,\mathrm{d}E}$$

Multi-exciton generation (MEG) limit (Ross & Nozik 1982):
$$\eta_{\mathrm{MEG}} = \frac{(h\nu - qV) + qV \cdot \min(1, h\nu/E_g)}{\mathrm{input}}$$

For high-energy photon ($h\nu \gg E_g$): each photon can generate $n_{\mathrm{e-h}} = h\nu/E_g$ pairs instead of 1.

| CIGS | 23.6% | Solar Frontier / 2023 |

# 問題 2：3 個根本分歧 (3 Fundamental Disagreements)

## 分歧 A：晶矽 (c-Si) vs 薄膜/鈣鈦礦 — 主流技術路線之爭

**Position A (c-Si dominant, ~95% market share 2024)**:
- 矽帶隙 1.12 eV 雖非最優但勝在 abundance (> 28% earth's crust by mass), non-toxicity, mature supply chain
- 2024 全球出貨: monocrystalline ≈ 75%, multi/PERC/PERT ≈ 10%, TOPCon ≈ 15%, HJT ≈ 2%
- LONGi, Jinko, Trina, JA Solar 四大龍頭佔全球產能 > 60%
- 优点: 寿命 > 25 年, 衰减率 < 0.55%/yr, manufacturing energy payback < 1 yr
- 缺点: 帶隙優化空間有限, 碳足跡 ~ 25–40 g CO₂/kWh, theoretical Auger limit only ~29.4% (Richter 2013)
- *Green, M.A. (2022). "Commercial progress and challenges for photovoltaics." Nature Energy, 7, 455–466.*

**Position B (Thin film + emerging)**:
- CdTe (First Solar): 模組效率 22.0–22.8%, 工作溫度系數僅 -0.32%/K (vs Si -0.40%/K), 大規模量產 $0.16/W
- 鈣鈦礦 + Si tandem: 實驗室 34.6–35.9% (Longi 2024, Oxford PV 2024)
- 理論極限: 鈣鈦礦 monolithic tandem 2T = 43–46%, 4T = 46–48%
- *Snaith, H.J. (2013). J. Phys. Chem. Lett.; Liu, J. et al. (2020). "Perovskite-silicon tandem solar cells." Joule.*
- 挑战: 鈣鈦礦長期穩定性 (humidity > 60% RH, > 1000 hr @ 85°C/85% RH target)

**Tension**: 商業規模生產追求 LCOE 最低; 物理極限追求效率最高. Perovskite/Si tandem 若能達到 30% 模組 + 30 年壽命, LCOE 可低至 $0.015/kWh (2025 預測 IEA). 為何 c-Si 仍主導? 因模組成本現已 < $0.10/W, 短期無換代誘因. 但 5–10 年後, tandem 可能 swing 整個 market. 額外 consideration: 鈣鈦礦含 Pb, 環保規管 (RoHS, WEEE) 喺歐盟可能限制其 deployment, 替代方案係 tin-based perovskite 但效率暫低 ~3 absolute %.

## 分歧 B：固定安裝 vs 追蹤系統 — 系統設計 KWh/$ 之爭

**Position A (Fixed-tilt)**:
- 1-axis trackers add ~$0.05–0.10/W hardware, but yield gain in mid-latitude 15–25% → LCOE still better at small scales
- O&M simplicity, lower failures (tracker motors: 1–2% annual failure), best for slopes > 5° or capped rooftops
- Cost: $0.65–0.95/W (固定 tilt)
- *Pelaez, S.A. & Deline, C. (2020). "Bifacial Photovoltaic Systems." NREL Technical Report NREL/TP-5K00-76600.*

**Position B (Trackers)**:
- Single-axis horizontal tracker (HSAT): ~ 20% yield gain in subtropical latitudes
- Dual-axis with backtracking: ~ 25–35% yield gain (高 latitude 更高)
- 阿根廷 Cauchari 1.2 GW project: 1-axis HSAT achieved 28% yield gain
- Cost: $0.85–1.10/W (含 tracker + foundation)
- *Pelaez, S.A. & Deline, C. (2020).* and *Sun, X. et al. (2021). "Optimization and performance of bifacial solar modules."*

### Q1. Derive the Shockley–Queisser (S-Q) detailed-balance limit and explain why the optimal bandgap ≈ 1.34 eV.

**Detailed answer**:

The detailed-balance limit (Shockley & Queisser 1961) assumes (i) every photon with hν ≥ E_g creates exactly one electron–hole pair, (ii) the cell emits blackbody radiation at T_cell ≈ 300 K, and (iii) only radiative recombination (no non-radiative loss). The framework balances absorbed photon flux against emitted photon flux under thermodynamic equilibrium with the sun.

The solar photon flux incident on the cell above E_g is given by Planck's law at T_sun = 5778 K:
$$N_{\mathrm{abs}} = \int_{E_g}^{\infty} \frac{2\pi}{h^3 c^2} \frac{E^2}{\exp(E/kT_{\mathrm{sun}}) - 1} \mathrm{d}E$$

The emitted flux from a 300 K cell into the same solid angle into free space:
$$N_{\mathrm{em}}(V) = \int_{E_g}^{\infty} \frac{2\pi}{h^3 c^2} \frac{E^2}{\exp[(E-qV)/kT_{\mathrm{cell}}] - 1} \mathrm{d}E$$

The current density is $J(V) = q[N_{\mathrm{abs}} - N_{\mathrm{em}}(V)]$. The open-circuit condition sets $N_{\mathrm{em}}(V_{\mathrm{oc}}) = N_{\mathrm{abs}}$, giving:
$$V_{\mathrm{oc}} = \frac{kT_{\mathrm{cell}}}{q}\ln\!\left[\frac{N_{\mathrm{abs}}}{N_{\mathrm{em}}(0)} + 1\right]$$

The maximum power output is found by solving $\partial P / \partial V = 0$ for $V_\mathrm{mpp}$.

Computing numerically for $E_g$ from 0.5 to 3.0 eV under AM1.5G (ASTM G173), the integral $\eta(E_g) = P_{\mathrm{max}}(E_g)/P_{\mathrm{in}}$ peaks at:

$$\boxed{E_g^{\mathrm{opt}} \approx 1.34\,\mathrm{eV}, \qquad \eta_{\mathrm{SQ}}^{\max} = 33.7\%}$$

The 1.34 eV optimum arises from two competing loss integrals:
- **Below 1.34 eV**: photons above $E_g$ thermalize, wasting $\Delta E = h\nu - E_g$ as heat (thermalization loss dominates).
- **Above 1.34 eV**: a larger fraction of the solar spectrum lies below $E_g$ and gets transmitted (transmission loss dominates).

Notably, single-crystalline Si at $E_g$ = 1.12 eV is below the optimum — every Si cell "pays a tax" of about 2–3 absolute % of efficiency for using the wrong bandgap. This motivates tandems (Si + perovskite 1.65 eV) and III–V (GaAs at 1.42 eV closer to optimum; GaAs single-junction holds 33.9% record).

The Auger and non-radiative loss extensions (Tiedje 1984; Richter 2013) further reduce the practical Si limit to ~29.4%, now widely accepted as the realistic Si ceiling. 2024 HJT Si cell achieved 27.8% (LONGi), only ~1.6% from this ceiling.

**Reference**: Shockley, W. & Queisser, H.J. (1961). "Detailed balance limit of efficiency of p-n junction solar cells." *J. Appl. Phys.*, 32(3), 510–519; Richter, A., Hermle, M. & Glunz, S.W. (2013). "Reassessment of the limiting efficiency for crystalline silicon solar cells." *IEEE J. Photovoltaics*, 3(4), 1184–1191.

### Q2. 什麼是光伏組件的溫度係數？為什麼�組件在高溫下效率降低？

**Detailed answer**:

光伏組件嘅 **temperature coefficient** 係 efficiency 隨 cell temperature 變化嘅 rate，常用 %/°C 表達。典型 mono-Si: $-0.35$ 至 $-0.45 \%/°\mathrm{C}$ (相對於 $P_{\max}$); CdTe ~ $-0.32 \%/°\mathrm{C}$; a-Si:H ~ $-0.20 \%/°\mathrm{C}$ (更穩定); HJT: $-0.25 \%/°\mathrm{C}$ (best among c-Si family).

**物理原理 (Physics)**:

從 equation $V_{\mathrm{oc}} = (nkT/q) \ln(I_L/I_0 + 1)$:
- $T$ 上升 → $kT$ 增大，理論上 $V_{\mathrm{oc}}$ 應該上升. 但 reverse saturation current $I_0$ 隨 $T$ 急劇增加: $I_0 \propto T^3 \exp(-E_g/kT)$. 由於 exponential dominates，$I_0$ 隨 $T$ 上升幾個數量級，導致 $V_{\mathrm{oc}}$ 反而**下降**. Differentiating rigorously:
$$\frac{\partial V_{\mathrm{oc}}}{\partial T} = \frac{V_{\mathrm{oc}} - E_g/q + 3kT/q}{T} \approx -2\,\mathrm{mV/K} \text{ for Si at } 300\,\mathrm{K}$$
- $I_{L}$ 随 $T$ 變化不大 (slight increase of ~+0.05 %/°C as absorption edge shifts to lower energy).
- FF 隨 $V_{\mathrm{oc}}$ 减少而降低，因為 $\mathrm{FF}_0 \approx (v_{\mathrm{oc}} - \ln(v_{\mathrm{oc}} + 0.72))/(v_{\mathrm{oc}}+1)$ 為 $v_{\mathrm{oc}}$ 嘅弱下降函數。
- Combined: $P_{\max} = I_\mathrm{mpp} V_\mathrm{mpp}$ drops at ~−0.40 %/°C.

**工程影響**: Hong Kong 夏天 panel surface temperature 可達 60–70°C (比 25°C STC 高 35–45°C). 假設 $-0.40 \%/°\mathrm{C}$, 夏天中午功率損失 ~ 14–18%. **解決方案**: (a) 安裝時增加 空氣間隙 5–10 cm, (b) BIPV-通風設計, (c) 反射塗層 (抗紅外), (d) 使用 low temperature coefficient 電池 (HJT = -0.25 %/°C, CdTe = -0.32 %/°C), (e) 採用 PV/T hybrid 強制水冷, (f) 智能 inverter 動態 derating 響應 weather forecast.

**Reference**: Green, M.A. (2014). *Solar Cells*, UNSW Press; Skoplaki, E. & Palyvos, J.A. (2009). "On the temperature dependence of photovoltaic module electrical performance: A review of efficiency/power correlations." *Solar Energy*, 83(5), 614–624.

### Q3. Describe the operating principle of MPPT controllers and compare Perturb-and-Observe (P&O) and Incremental Conductance (IncCond).

**Detailed answer**:

**P&O 原理**: Algorithm 每隔 Δt 改變 panel voltage 由 $V(k)$ 至 $V(k) + \Delta V$, 量度新 power $P(k+1)$, 同 $P(k)$ 比較. 如果 $P$ 上升 → 繼續同方向; 如果 $P$ 下降 → 改變方向. 一階差分: $\partial P / \partial V = I + V \cdot \partial I / \partial V$. 達到 MPP 時, $\partial P/\partial V \approx 0$ 但算法仍會 overshoot 兩邊, 形成 steady-state oscillation. 振蕩幅度 = $\Delta V \cdot 1$ (full step back-and-forth).

**IncCond 原理**: 用 $\partial I / \partial V = -I/V$ 作為 MPP exact condition (源自 $P = V I$, $\mathrm{d}P = 0$ at MPP). 達到 MPP 時，系統停止擾動，所以無 steady-state oscillation; 但需要更精確嘅 ADC 及時處理快速變化 (cloud edge ~ 100 ms).

**比較**:

| | P&O | IncCond |
|---|---|---|
| Steady-state oscillation | Yes (±ΔV) | No |
| Dynamic response to irradiance change | Slower (drift if ΔV too small) | Faster |
| Computational complexity | Low | Medium |
| Partial shadow | Can be stuck on local MPP | Better, with periodic restart |
| Implementation | Trivial | Need precise ADC + derivative |
| Tracking efficiency (clear day) | 96–98% | 98–99% |
| Tracking efficiency (cloud edge) | 90–95% | 95–98% |

**高級算法**: (a) **Variable step-size IncCond** (Liu 2008): step size $\Delta V \propto |\mathrm{d}P/\mathrm{d}V|$, 收斂快且無振蕩; (b) **Fuzzy-logic MPPT**: rule-based heuristic, ~99% efficiency but complex tuning; (c) **Sliding-mode control** (Matas et al. 2011): discontinuous control reaches MPP in finite time; (d) **Ripple Correlation Control (RCC)**: 利用 DC-DC converter 嘅 switching ripple 而無需注入 external perturbation; (e) **Model Predictive MPPT** (MPPT-MPC): predicts weather using LSTM NN, pre-empts MPP shifts.

**Reference**: 
- Esram, T. & Chapman, P.L. (2007). "Comparison of photovoltaic array maximum power point tracking techniques." *IEEE Trans. Energy Convers.*, 22(2), 439–449.
- Liu, F. et al. (2008). "A variable step size INC MPPT method." *IEEE Trans. Power Electron.*, 23(3), 1163–1170.
- Subudhi, B. & Pradhan, R. (2013). "A comparative study on maximum power point tracking techniques for photovoltaic systems." *IEEE Trans. Sustain. Energy*, 4(1), 89–98.
- Matas, J. et al. (2011). "Sliding-mode control of a boost converter for MPPT in PV systems." *IEEE Trans. Power Electron.*

### Q4. Why do PV modules need bypass diodes, and how many are required?

**Detailed answer**:

**問題根源**: Panel 內 ~ 60–72 cells 串聯. 當其中一部分被遮光 (樹影、鳥糞、落葉), 該 cell 變為無光照，反向偏壓, 並不斷由其他 cell 通過嘅電流被迫流過遮光 cell, 產生 hot spot. Hot spot 可達 150°C, 引致封装材料熔化、Laminate 裂紋、EVA 變黃, 最終火災風險 (UL 1703 標準要求 survive 5 min hot spot test).

The thermomechanical coupling: power dissipated in shaded cell $P_\mathrm{loss} = V_\mathrm{reverse} \cdot I_\mathrm{string}$. As $V_\mathrm{reverse}$ rises (towards the breakdown voltage ~ −13 V for Si cell), dissipation rises until thermal equilibrium is reached. The "multiplier" effect (1 shaded cell forces 20 illuminated cells to dump their current through it) creates local heating up to 200°C, causing permanent EVA browning and delamination.

**解決方案**: 並聯每 15–24 cells 嘅 bypass diode (typical 20 cells). 當 cell 被反向偏壓 > 1.5 V 時, diode 導通, 電流 "繞過" 遮光 cells, 整個 string 仍然可以輸出 power (雖然 power 降低 ~ 8% per shaded sub-string).

**數量原則**:
- 60-cell panel (modern std): 3 diodes，每 20 cells 一組
- 72-cell panel: 3 diodes, 每 24 cells 一組
- Half-cut cell (2020+ mainstream): 6 diodes, 每 20 half-cells 一組
- AC module / microinverter: 每個 panel 獨立 inverter, 無 need for block-level bypass
- Optimizer-based systems (e.g., SolarEdge): 每個 panel 獨立 MPPT, 自動 block-level power conversion → bypass diode remains safety device but power loss minimised

**功率代價**: 每個 bypass diode 漏電 ~ 1–5 mA (warm conditions), 影響只有 0.1%, 可以忽略. Schottky diode forward drop ≈ 0.4 V (vs standard Si 0.7 V) — Schottky preferred to minimize power loss per sub-string.

**Reference**: 
- Herrmann, W. et al. (1997). "Hot spot tests on crystalline silicon modules." *Proc. 26th IEEE PVSC*.
- Dhimish, M. et al. (2019). "Bypass diode characteristics." *Sol. Energy*, 188, 1106–1117.
- Silverman, T.J. et al. (2014). "Reliability of bypass diodes in PV modules." *Proc. 40th IEEE PVSC*.

### Q6. What is a PV/T (photovoltaic-thermal) hybrid system? How does it simultaneously enhance electrical and thermal output?

**Detailed answer**:

PV/T hybrid 系統將 PV 面板同 solar thermal collector 結合，喺同一個 panel area 內同時生產 electrical 與 thermal energy.

**設計種類**:
1. **Sheet-and-tube**: 黑色 thermal absorber sheet + pipe 陣列, 玻璃 cover
2. **Glazed vs unglazed**: Glazed = greenhouse effect, 較高溫度 (50–90°C) 但 electrical 損失; unglazed = ~ 30–50°C 
3. **Water vs air**: 水 比較好熱效率, 但冬季防凍
4. **Concentrated PV/T (CPV/T)**: 加入 Fresnel lens 或 parabolic dish, 同時產 electricity + high-temp heat (~150°C) for ORC

**熱回收**: Panel 背面冷卻水/空氣流過 → 帶走 normal 嘅熱廢 (cell 升高 20–40°C), 達到 dual benefit:
- 改善 electrical: 降溫有 -0.4 %/°C temperature coefficient. 降溫 10°C → +4% electricity.
- 提供熱水: 用於 residential 熱水 (~30–60°C); industrial process heat (~80–120°C); ORC-based combined cycle ~ 150°C.

**能效指標 (Exergy)**:
$$\eta_{\mathrm{ex}} = \eta_{\mathrm{elec}} + \eta_{\mathrm{thermal}} \cdot \left(1 - \frac{T_0}{T_{\mathrm{out}}}\right)$$

where $T_0 = 300\,\mathrm{K}$ (ambient) and $T_{\mathrm{out}}$ = thermal output temperature.

Unglazed PV/T: $\eta_{\mathrm{elec}}$ ≈ 14%, $\eta_{\mathrm{thermal}}$ ≈ 50%. Combined exergy efficiency ≈ 18–22%, 比 single PV ≈ 18% 提升.
