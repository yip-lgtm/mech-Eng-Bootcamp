# PHYS1110 Engineering Physics

**Phase 1 | Priority: ★★★**  
**Why critical**: Physical principles underlying mechanics, waves, electromagnetism and modern sensing.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Conservation Laws are Primary** — Energy, momentum and charge constrain every process.
2. **Fields Mediate Interactions** — Forces at a distance are described by fields.
3. **Wave–Particle Duality and Superposition** — Core of modern physics and signal thinking.
4. **Order of Magnitude and Dimensional Analysis** — First check of any model.
5. **Idealizations are Tools** — Point masses, frictionless surfaces, ideal gases are deliberate simplifications.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Classical First vs Early Modern Physics
- Classical builds intuition with everyday scales. Early modern prepares for quantum sensors and MEMS.

### Disagreement 2: Mathematical Rigour vs Conceptual Emphasis
- Rigour enables quantitative prediction. Concepts prevent blind calculation.

### Disagreement 3: Laboratory vs Lecture Priority
- Labs make abstract ideas concrete. Lectures cover more breadth efficiently.

## 3. 10 Questions that Distinguish Deep Understanding

1. Why is energy conserved in some systems but not others?
2. Physical content of Gauss’s law beyond the mathematical statement?
3. How does the concept of a field differ from action-at-a-distance?
4. Why do standing waves appear only at discrete frequencies in bounded systems?
5. Difference between phase velocity and group velocity?
6. What does it mean for two quantities to be conjugate in uncertainty principles?
7. How do you decide continuum vs discrete model?
8. Why can a static magnetic field do no work on a charged particle?
9. Role of boundary conditions in determining unique solutions to field equations?
10. Where does physics of noise and signal fundamentally limit robot sensor performance?
