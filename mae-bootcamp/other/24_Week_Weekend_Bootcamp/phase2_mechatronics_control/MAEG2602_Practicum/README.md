# MAEG2602 Engineering Practicum

**Phase 2 | Priority: ★★**  
**Why relevant**: Structured industrial or lab experience — the bridge between coursework and real engineering practice.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Learning by Doing Under Constraints** — Real projects have incomplete information, limited time and imperfect tools.
2. **Observation is a Skill** — Noticing how experienced engineers communicate, decide and recover from errors is as valuable as the technical task.
3. **Documentation and Handover Matter** — Work that cannot be understood or continued by others has limited value.
4. **Feedback Loops Accelerate Growth** — Seeking and acting on critique is the main mechanism of improvement.
5. **Professional Behaviour is Visible** — Punctuality, clarity, safety habits and respect shape how you are trusted.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Structured Training vs Sink-or-Swim Exposure
- Structured programmes protect learning goals. Immersion in real pressure teaches resilience and prioritisation.

### Disagreement 2: How Much Academic Credit for Workplace Learning
- Credit recognises genuine learning. Too much credit risks diluting academic standards.

### Disagreement 3: Technical Depth vs Breadth of Exposure During Practicum
- Depth in one system builds competence. Breadth of roles builds career orientation.

---

## 3. 10 Questions that Distinguish Deep Understanding

1. What was the single most important difference between classroom problems and the practicum tasks?
2. How did experienced engineers decide when “good enough” was enough?
3. What safety practices were non-negotiable, and why?
4. How was knowledge transferred when someone left or was absent?
5. What communication failures caused the most delay or rework?
6. How would you redesign one process you observed to reduce error or time?
7. What technical skill gap did the practicum reveal most clearly for you?
8. How did the organisation handle failure or near-misses?
9. What would you do differently on day one of a similar placement?
10. How does the practicum experience change your plan for the hybrid arm / soft gripper project?
