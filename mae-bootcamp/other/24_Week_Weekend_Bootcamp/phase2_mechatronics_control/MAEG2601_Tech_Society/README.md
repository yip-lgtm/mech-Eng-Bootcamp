# MAEG2601 Technology, Society and Engineering Practice

**Phase 2 | Priority: ★★**  
**Why relevant**: Ethics, societal impact and professional responsibility — the non-technical constraints that still shape every robot project.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Technology is Not Neutral** — Design choices embed values and distribute benefits and harms unevenly.
2. **Stakeholders Beyond the User** — Workers, bystanders, regulators and future generations are affected.
3. **Professional Responsibility is Personal** — Codes of ethics and legal duties apply to individual engineers, not only to companies.
4. **Unintended Consequences are Predictable in Outline** — History of automation shows recurring patterns of displacement, surveillance and dependency.
5. **Public Trust is a Design Constraint** — Safety, transparency and accountability determine whether a technology is adopted.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: How Much Social Science Belongs in an Engineering Degree
- Essential for responsible practice. Others argue it displaces scarce technical content.

### Disagreement 2: Individual Ethics vs Systemic Change
- Focus on personal integrity and whistleblowing. Focus on changing incentives, regulation and institutions.

### Disagreement 3: Optimism vs Caution about Automation
- Automation frees humans for higher work. Automation concentrates power and erodes livelihoods without deliberate policy.

## 3. 10 Questions that Distinguish Deep Understanding

1. What does “professional responsibility” mean when your employer’s interest conflicts with public safety?
2. How would you identify the stakeholders affected by a warehouse robot deployment?
3. Why is “the technology is just a tool” an incomplete ethical stance?
4. What historical lessons from industrial automation apply to today’s collaborative robots?
5. How should a soft-gripper designer think about dual-use (helpful vs harmful applications)?
6. What transparency obligations exist when an AI-based agent makes decisions that affect workers?
7. How do privacy and surveillance concerns arise in camera-equipped mobile robots?
8. What is the difference between compliance with law and ethical engineering?
9. How would you structure a risk assessment that includes social as well as technical risks?
10. In your hybrid system project, what is the most important non-technical risk or impact?
