# BMEG3420 Medical Robotics

**Phase 3 | Priority: ★★★**  
**Why critical**: Safety, constraints and applications of robots in medical contexts — high relevance to soft and compliant systems.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Safety is the Primary Constraint** — Patient and clinician safety dominate every design decision.
2. **Compliance and Softness are Features** — Soft and continuum robots reduce risk of tissue damage.
3. **Sterility, Biocompatibility and Regulations Shape Design** — Engineering must satisfy clinical and legal requirements.
4. **Human–Robot Collaboration is the Norm** — Surgeons remain in the loop; autonomy is carefully limited.
5. **Sensing in Unstructured, Deformable Environments is Hard** — Tissue deformation and limited visibility are core challenges.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: How Much Autonomy is Acceptable in the Operating Room
- Higher autonomy could reduce workload and variability. Many clinicians and regulators insist on strong human oversight.

### Disagreement 2: Rigid Precise Tools vs Soft Compliant Tools
- Rigid tools give precision and force transmission. Soft tools give safety and adaptability to anatomy.

### Disagreement 3: General-Purpose Platforms vs Procedure-Specific Devices
- Platforms amortise development cost. Specific devices can be better optimized for one clinical need.
