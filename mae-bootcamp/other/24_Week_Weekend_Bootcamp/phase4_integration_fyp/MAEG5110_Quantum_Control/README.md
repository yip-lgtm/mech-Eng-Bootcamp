## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Quantum State is a Vector in Hilbert Space** — Superposition and entanglement are structural, not classical mixtures.
2. **Measurement is Invasive** — Observation generally changes the state; control must account for this.
3. **Unitary Evolution is the Ideal Dynamics** — Open systems add decoherence and dissipation.
4. **Controllability Has a Quantum Version** — Lie-algebraic conditions generalise classical controllability ideas.
5. **Noise and Decherence are the Central Engineering Obstacles** — Coherence times limit what control can achieve.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: How Soon Quantum Control Becomes Relevant to Mainstream Engineers
- Near-term devices already need control engineers. Others see it as still specialised physics.

### Disagreement 2: Analogies from Classical Control vs Purely Quantum Formalism
- Classical intuition helps entry. Strict quantum formalism is required for correct results.

### Disagreement 3: Quantum Sensing vs Quantum Computing as the Nearer Application
- Sensing may deliver practical devices sooner. Computing is the higher-profile long-term goal.
