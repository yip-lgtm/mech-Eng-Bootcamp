## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Execution Under Constraint** — Time, parts, and knowledge are limited; prioritization and ruthless scope control are essential.
2. **Evidence Over Claims** — Every performance statement needs data, figures, or reproducible demos.
3. **Failure Analysis is Valuable** — Documented unsuccessful approaches and lessons learned strengthen the work.
4. **The Report and Demo are the Product** — Examiners primarily see what you present, not what you intended.
5. **Reproducibility and Clarity Win** — Someone else (or future you) should be able to understand and, ideally, rebuild the key results.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Polish the Existing System vs Keep Adding Features
- **Polish**: higher reliability and clearer story. **Features**: more impressive scope if they actually work.

### Disagreement 2: Quantitative Metrics vs Qualitative Demonstration
- **Metrics**: rigorous and comparable. **Demonstration**: often more convincing for integrated mechatronic systems.

### Disagreement 3: How Much Negative Result Reporting
- Some examiners value honest failure analysis. Others focus only on final successful outcomes.

## 3. 10 Questions that Distinguish Deep Understanding

1. What is the minimum set of experiments that convincingly validates your core contribution?
2. How do you present a partial failure so that it still demonstrates engineering competence?
3. What figures and tables must appear in the final report for a robotics FYP?
4. How would you structure a 15-minute demo of your hybrid arm + soft gripper system?
5. What questions are examiners most likely to ask about safety, limitations and future work?
6. How do you decide when to freeze features and focus purely on reliability and documentation?
7. What constitutes a ‘reproducible’ result in a hardware project?
8. How should code, CAD, data and videos be organized for submission and long-term archival?
9. In your project, what is the strongest single piece of evidence that the system works as claimed?
10. What would you do differently if you started the same FYP again with current knowledge?
