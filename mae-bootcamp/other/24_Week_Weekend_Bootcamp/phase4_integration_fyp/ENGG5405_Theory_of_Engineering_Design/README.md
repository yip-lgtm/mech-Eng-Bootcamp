# ENGG5405 Theory of Engineering Design

**Phase 4 | Priority: ★★★**  
**Why critical**: Design methodology, creativity and systematic processes — how to move from need to concept to validated solution.

---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Design is Iterative Decision-Making Under Uncertainty** — Not a linear sequence from specification to drawing.
2. **Function, Behaviour and Structure are Distinct** — Clarify what it must do before how it is built.
3. **Concept Generation Should be Divergent Before Convergent** — Explore many options before locking in.
4. **Evaluation Criteria Must be Explicit** — Otherwise selection is arbitrary or political.
5. **Documentation of Rationale is Part of the Design** — Future changes and reviews need the “why”.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Structured Methods (QFD, TRIZ, Axiomatic Design) vs Intuitive Expertise
- Structured methods improve consistency and teaching. Experienced designers often work more fluidly.

### Disagreement 2: How Early to Constrain Concepts with Manufacturing Reality
- Early constraints prevent unbuildable ideas. Premature constraints kill innovation.

### Disagreement 3: Individual Creativity vs Team Process Emphasis
- Individual insight still matters. Modern complex products are team outcomes.

## 3. 10 Questions that Distinguish Deep Understanding

1. What is the difference between a requirement and a concept?
2. How do you generate concepts systematically rather than waiting for inspiration?
3. What makes a good set of evaluation criteria for comparing robot gripper concepts?
4. How does the concept of a design space help you avoid local optima?
5. When should you freeze a concept and move to detailed design?
6. How do you capture design rationale so that a later engineer can change the design safely?
7. What is the role of prototyping in reducing uncertainty at each design stage?
8. How would you apply a structured method (e.g. morphological chart) to improve your soft gripper?
9. What failure of design process have you already observed in your hybrid project, and how would you prevent it?
10. How does design theory change the way you plan the next major upgrade of the system?
