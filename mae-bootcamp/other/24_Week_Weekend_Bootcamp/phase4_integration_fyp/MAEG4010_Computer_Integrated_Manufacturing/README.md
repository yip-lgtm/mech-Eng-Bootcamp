---

## 1. 5 Core Mental Models Every Expert Shares

1. **Integration of Information and Material Flow** — CIM is about connecting design, planning, control and physical processes.
2. **Flexibility vs Productivity Trade-off** — Dedicated lines are fast; flexible cells adapt; the right mix depends on volume and variety.
3. **Hierarchical Control** — From enterprise planning down to cell and machine control; each level has different time scales.
4. **Data is the Product of the Factory as Much as the Part** — Traceability, quality records and process data enable improvement.
5. **Humans Remain in the Loop** — Even highly automated systems need supervision, maintenance and exception handling.

---

## 2. 3 Places Experts Fundamentally Disagree

### Disagreement 1: Lights-Out Automation vs Human–Robot Collaboration
- Full automation maximises consistency. Collaboration retains human judgment and flexibility.

### Disagreement 2: Centralised vs Distributed Shop-Floor Control
- Centralised: easier optimisation. Distributed: more resilient and scalable.

### Disagreement 3: How Much Custom Robotics vs Standard Industrial Robots
- Custom: optimised for the task. Standard: lower cost, better support, faster deployment.
